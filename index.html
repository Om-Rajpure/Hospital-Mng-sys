Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding our booking procedure, doctors, and hospital services.

How do I book an appointment online? ▼

Simply navigate to our Appointment Page, fill in your details, select your preferred doctor and time slot, and submit. Your appointment is immediately saved in the system!

Can I choose a specific doctor and department? ▼

Yes! You can browse our specialist doctors on the Doctors Page and click "Book Appointment" next to any doctor to automatically pre-select their name and department.

Do I need to register before booking? ▼

While you can book directly, registering for an account allows your profile details to be auto-filled for future visits and gives you an organized dashboard of your appointment history.

What should I bring on the day of my appointment? ▼

Please bring a valid photo ID, your previous medical reports or prescription history if available, and arrive 10 minutes prior to your scheduled time slot.
